## A new kind: `chain`

The graph had four entity kinds for the City's things (artefact · creature · held · dispatch) and a `key` for the carrier. A PoAW chain is none of them: it is a mage instance's work as one page, every record addressed by its hash under one root, re-derived by the reader. It gets its own `NodeType`, `chain` (⛓️, Mage side: the work), placed by `vertex`/`bits`/`hammingWeight` from the first six bits of its ledger root, read Protection first, the same order as `lib/lattice-vertex.ts`.

- **`concept-poaw-chain`** · concept · *PoAW chain* · ⛓️ · the four readings (place, sign, sound, seal), the veil (lever names are the members'), the face that folds into a City Key as `chains`.
- **12 `chain-*` nodes** · each `instance_of` the concept; **4** also `anchors_to` an existing vertex node (spellweb holds 26 of the 64 vertex nodes; the other eight chains carry their vertex on the node and wait for theirs).

Work roots only, by Mitch's call: entry and leaf roots stay on the chain pages and the City star.
